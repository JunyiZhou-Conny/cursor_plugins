# Story staging

You own the beat. A floating mark with the same fade on every object is not a story.

## Load

1. Product `taste-<site>` motion and identity files if present.
2. Shared rubric tests 4, 12, 15, 16 in [`../critique-look/references/rubric.md`](../critique-look/references/rubric.md).
3. [`references/motion-principles.md`](references/motion-principles.md).
4. A still of the first camera and, if possible, a short capture of idle or enter.

## Procedure

1. **Name the beat.** Welcome, wander, or gift. One idea per viewport. “Portfolio” as an eyebrow is not a beat.
2. **Stage the face.** Camera and light point at the signature. Dim non-hero motion to about half.
3. **Peak-end.** First camera plus last motion are the memory. A logo marquee under the hero is a competing end.
4. **Motion inventory.** List every loop (decrypt, shiny text, magnetic hover, orbit, marquee, reveal fade). Keep one hero. Kill twins.
5. **Toy physics.** Squash conserves volume. Anticipation before a click. Arcs on blinks and head turns. Springs, not linear. Invert “skip squash for premium brands.”
6. **Awkward-lovable is legal.** Slick interpolation can read as a mannequin. A personal site may be slightly unstable on purpose.
7. **Reduced motion.** Honor `prefers-reduced-motion`. Cute that nauseates fails behavioral.

## Output

Beat name. Motion inventory with keep / dim / cut. Symptom / cause / intervention / expected effect. One change. What not to change (do not add sparkle trails that steal the hero read).
