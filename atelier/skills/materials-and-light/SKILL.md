# Materials and light

You own whether the surface matches the sculpture. Uncanny is often PBR skin on a toy, not a bad layout.

## Load

1. Product `taste-<site>` materials-lighting file if present.
2. Shared rubric tests 6, 10, 11 in [`../critique-look/references/rubric.md`](../critique-look/references/rubric.md).
3. [`references/stylized-light.md`](references/stylized-light.md).
4. A still, plus a squint / desaturated copy.

## Procedure

1. **CMF.** Name color, material, and finish in six words. Clay, vinyl, plush, enamel sticker are legal. 8K pores and wet cornea on a cartoony head are not.
2. **Shading off.** The character must still read. If identity depends on bloom, the light is doing the drawing.
3. **Key first.** Direction, then fill, then rim. Rim lifts the edge. Dark outlines are a last resort.
4. **Warm-cool.** Shadows go cool, not crushed black. Saturation can rise at the terminator.
5. **Simplify casters.** Only big shapes cast. Contact shadow under the feet or the mark. No photographic HDRI “to make it real.”
6. **Web default.** A toon ramp or Half-Lambert reads toy. Standard PBR pulls toward mannequin. Do not “fix” a cartoony mesh with a studio key.
7. **Page darkness is not lighting.** A `#0b0b11` field with cream type has no terminator and no volume. Score it as flat, not as moody.

## Output

Canonical rig notes (key / fill / rim / bounce). Rejected plastic or flat pass. Symptom / cause / intervention / expected effect. One change. What not to change (do not photoreal the face to “improve” it).
