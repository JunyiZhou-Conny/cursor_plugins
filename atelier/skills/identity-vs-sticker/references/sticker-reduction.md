# Sticker reduction

Adapt named tests from official creator rules. Link the pages. Do not copy LINE IP or Apple chrome.

Official hubs: [LINE creators](https://creator.line.me/en/guideline/) · [Apple Motion stickers](https://developer.apple.com/imessage/creating-stickers-with-motion/).

## Tests

- Transparent ground. The stamp sits on chat, not on a designed card.
- Legible rotated and scaled. Identity holds at tiny size.
- One idea per stamp. A conversational expression, not a collage.
- Reject objects-only (no character), photographs, poor visibility, ridiculously tall full-body, pale low-contrast.
- Alt text. An animated stamp is a short loop, not a film.
- Brand icons in orbit are not stickers. They are chrome. They cannot substitute for a face.

On this pack’s first consumer, skill-universe planets and a logo marquee are the usual false stickers.
