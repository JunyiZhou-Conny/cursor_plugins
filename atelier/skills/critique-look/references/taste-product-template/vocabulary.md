# Vocabulary

Bind the brief’s words to visible tests. Do not synonym-cycle.

| Word | Means on this site | Does not mean |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cute | Round mass, short limbs, readable face at 64px, Kindchenschema in the middle of the slider | Max kawaii. Generic baby. “Make it cuter” with no test |
| Warm | Cream and peach as *character* color, cool shadows, host-like staging | Cream-terracotta AI default on a serif landing page |
| Cartoony | Iconic face plus tactile toy materials. Simplexity. Detail budget | Photoreal pores. Dark-glass SaaS |
| Story-driven | One named beat per viewport. Peak-end on first camera and last motion | Extra paragraphs. A `PORTFOLIO` eyebrow |
| Coherent | Same CMF family on hero, stamp, favicon, merch | Matching Javis’s stylesheet |
| Identity | A body you can fill black and still name | A particle monogram, a skill orbit, a logo marquee |
| Sticker | A stamp that belongs to that body | Devicon planets doing the personality |
