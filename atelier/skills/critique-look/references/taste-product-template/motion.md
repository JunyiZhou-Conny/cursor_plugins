# Motion

## Hero beat

Welcome. The figure arrives or turns toward the visitor. Then the page is still enough to read the one-liner.

## Legal

- Squash with conserved volume.
- Anticipation on the primary button.
- Follow-through on hair or a single stamp.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` holds a still.

## Illegal on this site today (cut or dim)

- Name decrypt plus shiny-text sheen plus magnetic buttons plus logo marquee plus orbiting planets plus section reveal fades, all at once.
- Uniform 200 ms ease on every object.
- Bounce used as a default, not as squash.

Keep at most one of: enter, idle, hover. Dim the rest to 40–60 percent.
