# Critique protocol

Run this after you have a still. Do not argue from CSS.

## 1. Two isolated reads

Write a first-impression pass without the rubric. Then score the [17-test rubric](./rubric.md). If the two disagree, trust the pixels and name the conflict.

## 2. Design-specificity

Ask, in this order:

1. Could an unrelated product wear this unchanged?
2. Is the first eye-stop the character (or the one thing the brief named)?
3. Did we spend the free axis on an [anti-default](./anti-defaults.md)?
4. Remove one accessory (one sticker, one glow, one font, one orbit). What remains?
5. Is there one staged motion moment, or the same fade on every object?

## 3. Output shape

Every finding is four lines. No vibe adjectives.

- **Symptom.** What a stranger sees in two seconds.
- **Cause.** The craft row that failed (name the rubric test).
- **Intervention.** One concrete change.
- **Expected effect.** What the next still must show.

Severity:

- P0. Identity is missing or belongs to someone else.
- P1. The hero read fails at sticker, favicon, or far-scroll size.
- P2. Materials, light, or motion fight the sculpture.
- P3. Chrome hygiene.

Renormalize “minimalist” and “no bounce.” A sticker-cute brief may be maximal if each stamp earns its place. Squash with conserved volume is allowed.

## 4. Required closer

After the findings:

1. The 17 scores (0–4) and a total / 68.
2. The three biggest problems.
3. The single highest-leverage change.
4. What **not** to change.
