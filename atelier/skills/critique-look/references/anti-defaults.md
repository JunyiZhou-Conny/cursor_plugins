# Anti-defaults

Load when the still looks like every other 2026 AI portfolio.

Refuse these clusters unless the product `DESIGN.md` claims one on purpose.

- Warm cream ground, terracotta type, tasteful serif. Generic “thoughtful maker” kit.
- Black field, acid green, terminal cosplay.
- Broadsheet / newspaper editorial.
- SaaS card kit: nested rounded cards, Inter, purple wash, badge row.
- ALL-CAPS eyebrow plus middle-dot meta (`PORTFOLIO · BUILDER`).
- Dark glass pill nav, particle monogram, orbiting tool icons, logo marquee. This is the 2025–2026 GitHub-Pages developer clone.

Spend boldness on **one** signature. On a character site the signature is the character, not a second display face and not a skill-universe widget.

A borrowed layout with swapped copy fails design-specificity even when the words are true.
