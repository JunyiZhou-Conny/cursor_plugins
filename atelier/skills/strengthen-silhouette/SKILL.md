# Strengthen silhouette

You own the black-blob read. Color, stickers, and shaders come after.

## Load

1. Product `taste-<site>` silhouette and identity files if present.
2. Shared rubric tests 1, 2, 5, 6, 9 in [`../critique-look/references/rubric.md`](../critique-look/references/rubric.md).
3. A still. Prefer a desaturated copy and a filled-black trace of the hero.

## Procedure

1. **Trace.** Fill the hero (or mark) black on white. If you cannot name who it is, the silhouette failed.
2. **Size.** Check 16px, 64px, and hero. Hats, hair masses, and ears are the tools. Interior folds must echo the outer shape.
3. **Family.** Pick one dominant primitive. Circle for warm or cute. Add one contrast family only (a square coat, a triangular tuft). A second family that fights the first is noise.
4. **Pose.** Three-quarter plus a gesture. Default T-pose is lifeless. Avoid mirrored twins.
5. **Ground.** Contrast the mass against the field. A faint particle monogram on `#0b0b11` is a failed silhouette, not a subtle signature.
6. **Budget.** Drop nostrils, ear holes, and flyaways if they add noise. Hair is one or two clumps plus one break.

## Output

Symptom / cause / intervention / expected effect. Before and after traces. Rubric scores for tests 1, 2, 5, 6, 9. One change. What not to change (do not “IBM” the face into an 8-head grid; do not fashion-model the figure).
